What Information to Include in a Birthday Invitation

A well-crafted birthday invitation sets the tone for your party and ensures your guests have everything they need to celebrate with you smoothly. Here is what every complete invitation should feature:

  • Guest of Honor: Clear display of who is being celebrated, including nickname or family title.
  • Milestone Age (Optional): Highlighting major milestones like 1st, 16th, 18th, 21st, 30th, 40th, or 50th birthdays gives guests gift context.
  • Date and Exact Time: Clearly specify the day of the week, calendar date, and party start time or duration window.
  • Venue & Location: Provide the exact address, venue name, and helpful parking or entrance instructions.
  • Theme or Dress Code: Use the custom message box to notify guests of costume themes, cocktail attire, or casual poolside wear.
  • RSVP Deadline & Contact: State an RSVP date so you can finalize food, drinks, and seating accurately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Birthday Invitation Maker free to use?
Yes, our Birthday Invitation Maker is 100% free with no sign-ups, no accounts, no subscriptions, and zero watermarks on your downloaded invitation images. You can design as many invitations as you need.
Can I leave the age or RSVP fields blank?
Yes! Only the birthday person's name is required. All other fields — including age, event date, event time, venue location, custom message, and RSVP contact — are completely optional. If left blank, the invitation layout adapts smoothly without awkward gaps.
What size and resolution is the downloaded invitation image?
Clicking "Download High-Res PNG" generates an ultra-crisp 1200×1500 pixel image (standard 4:5 aspect ratio). This resolution is ideal for printing on 4×5 or 8×10 photo paper as well as posting in WhatsApp, Instagram, or email.
Are my party details or private contact information saved?
No. Everything is computed and rendered entirely client-side inside your browser via HTML5 Canvas. We never transmit, store, or log any party dates, addresses, phone numbers, or guest names.
How does sharing work on smartphones and computers?
On mobile devices supporting the Web Share API with images (iOS Safari, Android Chrome), clicking "Share Invite" opens your device's native sharing sheet to send the PNG directly. On desktop or unsupported browsers, it falls back to downloading the PNG image. You can also click "Copy Text" to copy the full invitation details for quick group chat pasting.
