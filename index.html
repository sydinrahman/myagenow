Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How does an age calculator work?
It calculates the calendar difference between your date of birth and the target date (today by default), breaking the duration down into completed calendar years, months, and remainder days according to the Gregorian calendar system.
Is this age calculator completely free?
Yes, 100% free with no registration, subscription, or software downloads required.
Can I calculate my age at a future or custom date?
Yes! You can change the “Age at Date” field to any future or past date to see how old you will be or were at a specific milestone.
How are leap years and February 29 handled?
The algorithm dynamically evaluates whether a year has 365 or 366 days. For individuals born on February 29th, during non-leap years their next birthday is calculated based on February 28th.
Does the website store or share my birth date?
No. All calculations run strictly client-side directly inside your web browser. No dates of birth or personal data are ever sent to, recorded, or stored on our servers.
