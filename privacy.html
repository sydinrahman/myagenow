Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This age calculator runs entirely on your device and does not require an account, login, or database connection.

  • All date-of-birth input and calculations are processed locally in your browser.
  • We do not store, log, or transmit your birth date to any server.
  • We do not use cookies for personal tracking or account management.
  • We may use anonymous analytics or basic site performance tools only when needed for service reliability, but we do not collect personal data for advertising or profiling.
  • Future AdSense integration, if enabled, will be implemented in a way that respects privacy and local-only calculator processing.

If you have questions about this policy or how the site is built, please contact us using the Contact page.
