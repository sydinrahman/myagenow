Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our top priority. Learn how myagenow keeps your calculations 100% private and client-side.

🔒
Key Takeaway: We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell your date of birth or any personal information. All age calculations execute locally in your web browser.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

When you enter your date of birth or target date into myagenow, all calculation logic runs strictly inside your local web browser using client-side JavaScript.

  • We do not transmit your birth date to any backend server or database.
  • We do not create user profiles, account records, or behavioral tracking files.
  • You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address to use the calculator.

2. Browser Storage & Cookies

We prioritize a clean, tracking-free browsing experience:

  • No Tracking Cookies: We do not use persistent tracking cookies or cross-site identifier cookies to monitor your browsing behavior.
  • Local State: Any temporary session inputs (such as form field states) remain solely within your active browser tab and clear when closed or reset.

3. Analytics & Advertising

To maintain site uptime, reliability, and free accessibility, we may periodically utilize lightweight standard web utilities:

  • Anonymous Performance Metrics: We may measure standard aggregate page views (e.g., bandwidth, page load speeds) without linking metrics to personal identities or birth dates.
  • Privacy-Respecting Monetization: If advertising networks (such as Google AdSense) display ads on this site, ad delivery will comply with privacy regulations and will never access your local calculator inputs.

4. Children’s Privacy

myagenow is a general-audience utility web tool suitable for users of all ages. Because we do not collect personal information from any user, we do not knowingly solicit or store data from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new site enhancements, technical standards, or regulatory guidelines. Revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

6. Contact & Questions

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please visit our Contact Page or reach out directly to hello@myagenow.net.
