Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Calculating Age on Any Date

How do I calculate my age on a specific date?
Simply enter your date of birth in the first field, select the target date you want to measure against (in the past or future) in the second field, and click "Calculate Age on Date". The calculator instantly yields your exact age in completed years, months, and remainder days, alongside the total number of days between both dates and the target date's day of the week.
Can I calculate my age on a future date?
Yes! You can choose any future date—such as a planned retirement date, milestone anniversary, or future event—to see exactly how old you will be on that day. The tool dynamically phrases the results in future tense ("you will be").
How does the calculator handle leap years and February 29th?
The algorithm follows the Gregorian calendar rules, taking into account differing month lengths and leap years (366 days). For individuals born on February 29th, calculations evaluated on non-leap target years seamlessly account for February 28th.
Can the date of birth be after the calculation date?
No. You cannot calculate an age prior to birth. If a date of birth is selected that occurs after the target date, a friendly error message will guide you to choose a target date that is equal to or after the birth date.
Is my birth date kept private and safe?
Yes, 100%. All calculations are processed locally inside your web browser. Neither your birth date nor the target date is ever stored, transmitted, or logged on any server.
