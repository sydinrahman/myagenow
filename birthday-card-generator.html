Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Birthday Card Generator completely free to use?
Yes, our Birthday Card Generator is 100% free with no sign-ups, no hidden subscriptions, and no watermarks on your downloaded cards. You can design and export as many personalized cards as you want.
Can I download the card as a high-resolution image?
Yes! Clicking the "Download High-Res PNG" button exports a high-definition 1200×1500px portrait image. This resolution is ideal for both crisp digital sharing (WhatsApp, Instagram, AirDrop) and standard greeting card photo printing (4×5 or 8×10 inch prints).
Does the card generator calculate the recipient's turning age?
Yes. When you provide the recipient's date of birth and check "Show turning age milestone badge", our algorithm calculates how old they are turning on their upcoming birthday and renders an eye-catching milestone badge (e.g., "Turning 30!").
Are my names, messages, or dates stored anywhere?
No. All card generation, text formatting, and canvas image rendering take place strictly inside your browser's local sandbox. No names, messages, or dates are ever sent to or stored on any server.
How does the Share button work on phones and computers?
On mobile devices (iOS Safari, Android Chrome) that support the Web Share API with image files, clicking Share opens your native sharing sheet so you can directly send the PNG file to WhatsApp, Messages, or social apps. On desktop or unsupported browsers, it gracefully downloads the high-res card image to your downloads folder.
