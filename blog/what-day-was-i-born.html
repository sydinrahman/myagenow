What Day of the Week Was I Born?

Published on October 10, 2026 • By myagenow Team

Have you ever wondered whether you were born on a energetic Monday, a restful Sunday, or a bustling Friday? Most people know their date of birth, but fewer know the exact day of the week they arrived into the world.

How Do Algorithms Calculate the Day of the Week?

Determining the day of the week for any past calendar date is based on mathematical algorithms such as Zeller's Congruence or the Doomsday Rule.

Because standard years have 365 days (52 weeks + 1 extra day), your birthday advances by 1 day of the week each non-leap year, and by 2 days following a leap year.

Practical Example

Date of Birth: January 15, 2002
Calculation Result: Tuesday
