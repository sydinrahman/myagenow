What Day of the Week Was I Born?

Written by MyAgeNow • Updated October 10, 2026

You probably know your birthday.

But do you know what day of the week you were born?

Maybe it was a Monday.

Maybe a Saturday.

Or perhaps you've never checked.

Your birth date contains more information than just the day, month, and year. It can also tell you exactly which day of the week it was.

How can you find the day you were born?

The day of the week is determined by your complete birth date.

For example, if you know:

Month + Day + Year

you can calculate the corresponding weekday.

The seven possible days are:

  • Monday
  • Tuesday
  • Wednesday
  • Thursday
  • Friday
  • Saturday
  • Sunday

Every date belongs to exactly one of these days.

Why can't I just guess it?

The calendar follows a repeating pattern, but the pattern isn't exactly the same every year.

A normal year contains 365 days.

A leap year contains 366.

That extra day changes how dates line up with the days of the week in following years.

So your birth year matters.

What does a leap year have to do with it?

Leap years include February 29.

That extra day affects the calendar's progression.

When calculating a historical date, a proper calculation needs to account for the calendar rules that were in effect.

That's why entering your complete birth date is important.

Is it useful to know?

There's no practical requirement to know which weekday you were born on, but it's a fun piece of personal information.

You might use it for:

  • Birthday trivia
  • Family conversations
  • Personal curiosity
  • Birthday planning
  • Learning more about your birth date

You can even ask your family:

"Did I really arrive on a Sunday?"

A fun birthday fact

Once you know the weekday of your birth, you can compare it with future birthdays.

Your birthday doesn't necessarily fall on the same weekday every year.

For example, a birthday that falls on a Tuesday one year might fall on a Wednesday the next year.

Leap years can affect this pattern as well.

Final thoughts

Your birthday is more than just a date on the calendar.

It can tell you your age, your next birthday, and even the day of the week when you were born.

If you've never checked, why not find out?
