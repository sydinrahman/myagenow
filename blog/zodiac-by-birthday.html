What Is Your Zodiac Sign Based on Your Birthday?

Published on October 18, 2026 • By myagenow Team

Your birth date determines your western tropical zodiac sign based on the position of the Sun relative to celestial longitudes at the time of your birth. Each of the 12 zodiac signs spans roughly a 30-day period across the calendar year.

Zodiac Sign Date Ranges

Here are the standard tropical zodiac date ranges and symbols:

  • Aries ♈: March 21 – April 19
  • Taurus ♉: April 20 – May 20
  • Gemini ♊: May 21 – June 20
  • Cancer ♋: June 21 – July 22
  • Leo ♌: July 23 – August 22
  • Virgo ♍: August 23 – September 22
  • Libra ♎: September 23 – October 22
  • Scorpio ♏: October 23 – November 21
  • Sagittarius ♐: November 22 – December 21
  • Capricorn ♑: December 22 – January 19
  • Aquarius ♒: January 20 – February 18
  • Pisces ♓: February 19 – March 20

What About Cusp Birthdays?

If you were born on the start or end date of a sign range (a "cusp"), your sign depends on the exact time of solar transition in your birth year.
