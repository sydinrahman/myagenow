How Many Days Until My Birthday?

Published on October 7, 2026 • By myagenow Team

Whether you are planning a trip, organizing a party, or simply excited to celebrate another trip around the sun, counting down the days to your birthday is always exciting. But how do you accurately figure out how many days, weeks, or months are left until your next birthday?

How Birthday Countdowns Work

To calculate how many days remain until your next birthday:

  1. Determine your birthday in the current calendar year.
  2. If that date has already passed this year, set your target date to your birthday in the next calendar year.
  3. Count the exact number of days between today's date and that target birthday date.

Example Birthday Countdown Calculation

Today's Date: October 7, 2026
Next Birthday: December 25, 2026
Days Remaining: 79 Days (or 11 Weeks and 2 Days).

Special Case: Leap Year Birthdays (February 29)

If you were born on a leap day (February 29), calculating your next birthday requires special consideration during non-leap years. To learn how leap years affect your age and birthday calculations, check out our guide on How Does a Leap Year Affect Your Birthday?.
