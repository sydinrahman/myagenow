How Many Days Until My Birthday?

Written by MyAgeNow • Updated October 7, 2026

There's something fun about knowing exactly how long you have to wait for your next birthday.

Maybe it's only a few days away. Maybe you have several months to go.

Either way, a birthday countdown gives you an exact answer to one simple question:

How many days until my birthday?

How does a birthday countdown work?

A birthday countdown compares today's date with the date of your next birthday.

For example, imagine your birthday is December 20 and today is October 5.

The calculator counts the number of days between those two dates.

If your birthday has already passed this year, the countdown looks ahead to your birthday next year.

What happens when your birthday has already passed?

This is important.

Suppose your birthday is March 10, but today is October 5.

Your March birthday has already happened this year.

So the next birthday is March 10 of the following year.

The countdown should therefore calculate the number of days from October 5 to the next March 10.

Why can the number of days change?

The countdown changes every day.

For example:

100 days → 99 days → 98 days → 97 days

Your birthday gets one day closer every day.

The exact number can also depend on how many days are in the months between today and your birthday.

What about leap years?

Leap years add an extra day to the calendar.

That means a birthday countdown that crosses February can be affected by whether the relevant year contains February 29.

A proper date calculation should account for the actual calendar rather than assuming every year has exactly 365 days.
