How Old Am I? Calculate Your Exact Age

Written by MyAgeNow • Updated October 6, 2026

Have you ever wondered, "How old am I exactly?"

Most of us know our age in years, but that's not always the complete answer. Depending on your birthday and today's date, you can also calculate your age in months and days.

For example, someone might be 25 years, 4 months, and 12 days old rather than simply saying they are 25.

Here's how it works.

How is your age calculated?

Your age is calculated by comparing your date of birth with the current date.

The calculation looks at three main parts:

  • Completed years
  • Completed months after your last birthday
  • Remaining days

For example, if someone was born on January 15, 2001, and today's date is May 27, 2026, their age is:

25 years, 4 months, and 12 days.

The exact result depends on the two dates being compared. If you want to compare other historical or future milestone dates, learn how in our guide to calculating age between two dates.

Why isn't calculating age just subtraction?

It might seem like you can simply subtract the birth year from the current year.

For example:

2026 − 2001 = 25

But that doesn't always give you the correct age.

If your birthday hasn't happened yet this year, you're still one year younger than the simple subtraction suggests.

Months and days also make the calculation more complicated because months don't all have the same number of days.

That's why an exact age calculation compares the complete dates.

What if my birthday hasn't happened yet?

This is one of the most common sources of confusion.

Imagine someone was born on December 10, 2005.

If today's date is October 5, 2026, they haven't reached their birthday yet.

So they are still 20, not 21.

Once December 10 arrives, they become 21.

What about leap years?

Leap years can also affect date calculations.

A leap year has 366 days instead of 365, because February has an extra day.

An accurate age calculator needs to account for those calendar differences when calculating the exact difference between two dates.
