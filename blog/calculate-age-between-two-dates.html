How to Calculate Age Between Two Dates

Written by MyAgeNow • Updated October 8, 2026

Sometimes you don't want to know someone's age today.

You want to know the exact age between two specific dates.

For example:

How old was I on January 1, 2020?

Or:

How much time passed between my birthday and another date?

This is where calculating the difference between two dates becomes useful.

What information do you need?

You only need two dates:

Start date: the person's date of birth or another starting date.

End date: the date you want to calculate the age or difference up to.

For an age calculation, the start date is usually the person's birthday.

Simple example

Imagine someone was born on:

June 15, 2000

And we want to calculate their age on:

October 5, 2026

The result is:

26 years, 3 months, and 20 days.

The calculation doesn't simply subtract the years.

It also considers the months and remaining days.

Why is date calculation tricky?

At first, it seems easy.

But there are several things to consider:

1. Different month lengths

Months don't all contain the same number of days.

Some have 30 days, some 31, and February has fewer.

2. Leap years

A leap year contains an extra calendar day.

This matters when the date range crosses February.

3. Whether the birthday has occurred

If you're calculating someone's age during a year when their birthday hasn't happened yet, you can't simply subtract the two years.

Years, months, and days

An exact age calculation usually expresses the result as:

Years + Months + Days

For example:

18 years, 7 months, 4 days

This is often more useful than simply saying:

18 years

because it provides the complete date difference.

Can I calculate it manually?

Yes, but manual calculations can become confusing when the two dates are far apart.

You need to account for:

  • Month lengths
  • Leap years
  • Birthday timing
  • Borrowing days between months
  • Whether the end date has already passed the birthday

That's why an age calculator can save time.
