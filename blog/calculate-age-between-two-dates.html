How to Calculate Age Between Two Dates

Published on October 8, 2026 • By myagenow Team

Calculating age is usually done relative to today's date. However, there are many situations where you need to calculate someone's age on a specific date in the past or future—such as determining your age on your graduation day, retirement date, or historical event.

Common Use Cases for Date-to-Date Calculations

Calculating the exact duration between two dates is essential for:

  • Historical & Ancestry Research: Finding exact lifespan durations for genealogy records.
  • Retirement & Career Planning: Knowing your exact age when reaching pension eligibility or service milestones.
  • Event Planning: Determining how old a child will be on the first day of school or an upcoming family vacation.

The Step-by-Step Formula

To find the difference between a Start Date and an End Date:

1. Calculate Years

Subtract the start year from the end year. Adjust down by 1 if the end month/day precedes the start month/day in the calendar year.

2. Calculate Months & Days

Adjust for uneven month lengths (28, 29, 30, or 31 days) when borrowing days from the previous month.
