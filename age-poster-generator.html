Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an age poster?
An age poster is a personalized piece of graphic art summarizing your journey through time. It displays your exact chronological age in years, months, and days, along with fascinating life statistics like total days and weeks lived, astrological zodiac sign, countdown to your next birthday, and your next major age milestone.
Is the Age Poster Generator completely free to use?
Yes, our Age Poster Generator is 100% free with no sign-ups, no hidden fees, and zero watermarks on your downloaded poster. You can design and export as many personalized posters as you want.
What resolution is the downloaded poster image?
The poster downloads as a 1200×1500 pixel PNG (standard 4:5 portrait aspect ratio). This high resolution is sharp enough for smartphone lock screens, social media sharing, and physical prints in standard frame sizes such as 8×10, 12×15, 16×20, or 24×30 inches.
Are my birth date and personal details kept private?
Yes. All calculations and graphics rendering are processed 100% locally inside your web browser. No dates of birth or names are transmitted to or stored on our servers.
How does the Copy Link feature work?
Clicking "Copy Link" generates a secure, shareable URL with your selected name, birth date, title, template, and color theme encoded in the query parameters. When a friend or family member opens the link, the page automatically renders your exact poster configuration.
