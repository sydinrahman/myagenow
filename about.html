About MyAgeNow

A simple, privacy-focused tool for understanding your age, birthdays, and important date milestones.

MyAgeNow was created to make age and date calculations simple. Instead of doing the math yourself, enter your date of birth and get your exact age in years, months, and days instantly.

What MyAgeNow does

Exact Age
Calculate your age in years, months, and days.

Birthday Countdown
See how long until your next birthday.

Life Statistics
Explore useful statistics such as days lived and upcoming milestones.

Date & Birthday Information
Learn more about birthdays, dates, zodiac signs, and calendar calculations.

Why we built it

Age calculation sounds simple, but getting an exact result can involve different month lengths, leap years, and calendar rules.

MyAgeNow was built to make that process quick and easy for everyone.

Your date of birth stays private

MyAgeNow is designed with privacy in mind. Your date of birth is used to perform the calculation in your browser. We don't need you to create an account or provide personal information just to calculate your age.

Our goal

Our goal is simple: build small, useful tools that are fast, easy to understand, and enjoyable to use.
